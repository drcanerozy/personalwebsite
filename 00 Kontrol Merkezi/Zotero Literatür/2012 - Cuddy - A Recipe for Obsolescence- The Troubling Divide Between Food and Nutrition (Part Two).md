## 📝 4. Orijinal Özet (Abstract)
> This paper is the second in a series examining the emerging disconnect between “food” and “nutrients” in dietetics curriculum.  Part one was described from the perspective of a budding dietitian, newly embarking on her professional journey.  Part two comes from the perspective of a dietitian with over twenty years of professional experience in several practice areas.
