## 📝 4. Orijinal Özet (Abstract)
> Recent advances in pharmacotherapies that promote appetite suppression have shown remarkable weight loss. Therapies targeting energy expenditure lag behind, and as such none have yet been identiﬁed to be safe and efﬁcacious for sustaining negative energy balance toward weight loss. Multiple energy dissipating pathways have been identiﬁed in adipose tissue and muscle. The molecular effectors of some of these pathways have been identiﬁed, but much is still left to be learned about their regulation. Understanding the molecular underpinnings of metabolic inefﬁciency in adipose tissue and muscle is required if these pathways are to be therapeutically targeted in the context of obesity and obesity-accelerated diseases.
