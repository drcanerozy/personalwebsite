## 📝 4. Orijinal Özet (Abstract)
> The adverse cardiometabolic effects of ultra-processed foods (UPF) cannot be explained solely by hyper-palatability or nutrient profiling. We review mechanistic pathways centered on physical food matrix disruption, accelerated transit and absorption kinetics, and synergistic additive interactions. Synthetic emulsifiers (e.g., CMC, P80) degrade the protective intestinal mucin barrier, inducing subclinical endotoxemia and systemic microinflammation.
