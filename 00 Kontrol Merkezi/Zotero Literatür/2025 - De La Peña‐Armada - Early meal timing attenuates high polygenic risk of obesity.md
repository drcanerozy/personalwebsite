## 💡 2. Ana Çıkarımlar & Bulgular
1. Objective: We examined whether meal timing is associated with long-term weightloss maintenance and whether meal timing interacts with a genome-wide polygenic score (PRS-BMI) on body weight-related outcomes.
2. We then examined the interaction of meal timing with 97 BMI-related single-nucleotide polymorphisms on obesity outcome.
