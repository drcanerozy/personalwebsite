## 🔴 Makale Bulguları (Kırmızı)
- "The analysis result yielded a statistically significant positive effect, with a weighted average effect size of 0.924 (95 % CI [0.612, 1.235], p < .01). This positive effect was aligned with prior meta-analysis and related systematic review (e.g., Sun & Zhou, 2024; Zheng et al., 2023). It suggests that students engaged in the learning process with the support of AI technologies had better ac­ ademic achievement than their peers in traditional learning environ­ ments by an average of 0.924 standard deviations."
