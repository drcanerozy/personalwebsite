## 💡 2. Ana Çıkarımlar & Bulgular
1. Background: Patients with critical illness may present with disease‐related malnutrition upon intensive care unit (ICU) admission.
2. They are at risk of development and progression of malnutrition over the disease trajectory because of inflammation, dysregulated metabolism, and challenges with feeding.
