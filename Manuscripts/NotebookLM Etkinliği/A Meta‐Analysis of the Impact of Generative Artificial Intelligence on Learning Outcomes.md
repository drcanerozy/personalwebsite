## 🟢 Atıf Yapılabilir (Yeşil)
- "With regard to rapid development in education technology, generative AI has increasingly found its way into the education sector (O'Dea 2024). Therefore, teachers need to pay more attention toward using generative AI in an efficacious manner for improving learning performance and abilities among students (Kurtz et al. 2024)" (Sayfa 1) [PDF'de Gör](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/MSW74H6G?page=1)- "Current evidence shows that GAI offers dynamic personalised education as a result of learner characteristics and requirements, essentially subverting conventional instructor-based teaching models (Kong and Yang 2024). The highly interactive nature of real-time responses offered by generative AI addresses learners' practical requirements for obtaining knowledge and interactivity in communication, hence drastically altering patterns of interaction in different learning environments (Jauhiainen and Guerra 2023). In addition, GAI can track learners' performance in real-time, creating personalised problems based on their knowledge features, traces and error modes and frequencies, hence helping learners acquire mastery in certain knowledge points (Pesovski et al. 2024)." (Sayfa 2) [PDF'de Gör](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/MSW74H6G?page=2)- "Additionally, the literature review by Ahmed et al. (2024) supports that GAI enhances learning efficiency, fosters innovative learning approaches and increases student engagement.  Furthermore, this conclusion corroborates the findings of Shi et al. (2024), who demonstrated that GAI-supported contextual interactive teaching significantly improves students' cognitive, skill-based and affective learning outcomes" (Sayfa 14) [PDF'de Gör](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/MSW74H6G?page=14)- "Previous research shows that GAI has a significant impact on low-experience learners' efficiency, assisting them in filling experience gaps in a timely manner through real-time recommendation and best practice demonstration (Brynjolfsson et  al. 2025)." (Sayfa 15) [PDF'de Gör](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/MSW74H6G?page=15)- "it should be noted that students may need to invest more time and energy in verifying the accuracy of AI-generated content, and may even fall into the problem of information overload (Ahmed et al. 2024)." (Sayfa 16) [PDF'de Gör](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/MSW74H6G?page=16)

## 🔴 Makale Bulguları (Kırmızı)
- "The results indicate a combined effect size of 0.68 (p<0.001), suggesting that GAI has a moderately high positive impact on learners' learning outcomes, meaning its application contributes to improving learning performance. Specifically, GAI demonstrates a significant effect on the cognitive dimension (g=0.795, p<0.05) and the competency dimension (g=0.711, p<0.05), while its impact on the affective dimension (g=0.507, p<0.05) is moderate." (Sayfa 14) [PDF'de Gör](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/MSW74H6G?page=14)- "GAI has a large positive effect in mathematics and natural sciences and in humanities, for which higher effect sizes are noted. Computer science and medical and nursing education have smaller effect sizes, indicating a moderate effect." (Sayfa 14) [PDF'de Gör](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/MSW74H6G?page=14)- "With regard to pre-existing knowledge, GAI has positive impacts on low-experience and high-experience learners alike, none showing notable variation." (Sayfa 15) [PDF'de Gör](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/MSW74H6G?page=15)- "From the perspective of Cognitive Load Theory, this study found that generative artificial intelligence significantly reduced the external cognitive load of learners, showing a relatively high cognitive dimension effect value (g=0.795)." (Sayfa 15) [PDF'de Gör](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/MSW74H6G?page=15)- "Based on the Self-Regulated Learning (SRL) theory, this study found that the real-time feedback and adaptive resources of GAI significantly enhanced the learners' abilities (g=0.711), confirming the promoting effect of external scaffolds on ability development in the self-regulated learning theory" (Sayfa 16) [PDF'de Gör](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/MSW74H6G?page=16)- "Therefore, this study highlights the necessity of the ‘autonomy balance under technology mediation’ mechanism in the SRL theoretical framework to ensure that learners can maintain the necessary self-motivation while receiving timely support." (Sayfa 16) [PDF'de Gör](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/MSW74H6G?page=16)

## 🔵 Conclusions / Sonuç (Mavi)
- "Thus, in instructional design, GAI implementation should be calibrated relative to learners' cognitive traits in order for it to be effective in various learning phases." (Sayfa 15) [PDF'de Gör](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/MSW74H6G?page=15)- "Theoretically, GAI's adaptability in such areas is well-supported by Cognitive Load Theory and Adaptive Learning Theory mechanisms.  GAI optimises information presentation and minimises extraneous cognitive load, which allows students to concentrate on the construction of essential knowledge and utilise adaptive algorithms that align with students' knowledge level and requirements specifically. This maintains stable learning support under various differences in teaching cycles, knowledge categories, or students' prior knowledge level." (Sayfa 15) [PDF'de Gör](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/MSW74H6G?page=15)
