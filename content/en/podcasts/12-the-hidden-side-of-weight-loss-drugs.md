Criticized for living with obesity, yet judged for utilizing pharmacology to treat it. What happens when societal stigma clashes with metabolic breakthroughs?

In this episode of our GLP-1 series, we explore the psychosocial realities, covert usage, illicit compound sourcing, and how interpersonal attitudes shift overnight after weight reduction.

🎧 **Listen on Spotify:** [The Hidden Side of Weight Loss Drugs (Episode #12)](https://open.spotify.com/episode/3cMT58kTqaADyVX76Y0rJW)

**Key Discussion Points:**
- The psychosocial paradox: Stigma of obesity vs. stigma of pharmacotherapy
- The emergence of unregulated gray/black markets for compounded peptide formulations
- Why do people perceive medical weight management as "cheating"?
- Sudden societal shifts in interpersonal treatment post-weight loss
- Emerging neurobehavioral observations: Dopaminergic tone, cravings, and addictive behaviors
