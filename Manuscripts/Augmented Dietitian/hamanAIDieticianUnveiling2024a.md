The primary objective of this research is to scrutinize and comprehensively assess the accuracy and reliability of the nutritional information provided by ChatGPT. The investigation aims to validate the correctness of the nutritional values given by the AI model for a diverse range of foods and meals. Accuracy, in this context, is of critical importance. Inaccurate or inflated nutritional values, if provided by the AI, could derail diet plans, rendering them ineffective or counterproductive.
