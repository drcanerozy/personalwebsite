## 💡 2. Ana Çıkarımlar (Key Takeaways)

## 📝 4. Orijinal Özet (Abstract)
> Periodic cycles of fasting-mimicking diets (FMD) promote multi-system metabolic benefits and tissue regeneration. Here we show that FMD cycles induce autophagy-driven clearance of damaged organelles in pancreatic islets followed by Ngn3-driven beta-cell progenitor activation. Critically, functional beta-cell differentiation and robust insulin secretion restoration occur specifically during the refeeding phase.
