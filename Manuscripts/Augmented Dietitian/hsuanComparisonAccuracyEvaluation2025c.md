Methods: "To evaluate the accuracy and clinical applicability of AI-assessed nutrition data by comparing outputs from five AI models with professional dietitian estimations and labeled nutrition factsEight ready-to-eat convenience store meals were analyzed. Four experienced dietitians independently estimated the meals’ calories, macronutrients, and sodium content based on measured food weights. Five AI chatbots were queried multiple times with identical input prompts to assess intraand inter-assay variability. All results were compared to the official nutrition labels to quantify discrepancies and cross-model consistency."
Findings: "Dietitian estimations showed strong internal consistency (CV < 15%), except for fat, saturated fat and sodium (CVs up to 33.3 ± 37.6%, 24.5 ± 11.7%, and 40.2 ± 30.3%, respectively)Sodium values were consistently underestimated across all AI models, with CVs ranging from 20% to 70%.
