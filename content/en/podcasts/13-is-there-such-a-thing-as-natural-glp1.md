Your body already produces its own GLP-1. The issue isn't production — an enzyme in your gut degrades it within roughly two minutes. Pharmacotherapies simply prolong that short window.

Do viral "nature's Ozempic" drinks, oat concoctions, or berberine supplements replicate this effect? While some rely on valid biological pathways, their bioavailability is minimal; others offer nothing beyond short-lived fullness.

In this episode of Konsantre Podcast, we dissect what the "natural" label actually entails, what has physiological grounding, and what is purely marketing hype.
