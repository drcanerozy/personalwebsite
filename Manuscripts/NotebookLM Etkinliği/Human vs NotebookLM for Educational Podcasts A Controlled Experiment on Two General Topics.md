## 🔴 Makale Bulguları (Kırmızı)
- "Results reveal that, within this small pilot study, LLM-generated podcasts lead to higher comprehension scores and are rated more topically focused than human-generated podcasts, with positive but not always significant trends in clarity and engagement. These findings suggest that LLM-generated podcasts can serve as a scalable and effective alternative to human-generated podcasts in certain educational contexts, offering new possibilities for personalized, accessible learning in digital education environments."
