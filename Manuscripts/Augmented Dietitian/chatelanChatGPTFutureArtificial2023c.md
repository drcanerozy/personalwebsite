## 🟡 Genel Bilgiler (Sarı)
- "AI chatbots for assistance in their daily work whereas pessimistic ones will consider them as tools that could harm our profession and partly replace us. This includes registered dietitian nutritionists (RDNs) and nutrition dietetics technicians (NDTs). This article guides those working in public health, clinical settings, and academia, among other settings, to perceive the possible opportunities and risks of using ChatGPT in their practice." ([Sayfa 1](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/9LREN8TF?page=1&annotation=9JWXHZ3A))- "For RDNs and NDTRs, ChatGPT may lead to missed opportunities for inperson contact and care by patients/ clients thinking that AI chatbot advice is good enough. ## 🔵 Sonuç / Conclusions (Mavi)
- "AI chatbots can assist RDNs and NDTRs, but cannot be used as a replacement for their expertise, judgment, and soft skills. We should be aware of this technology and promote our profession and our competencies over ChatGPT to the general public, communities, and patients/clients. In parallel, we should reinforce our profession to provide high-quality, accurate, and tailored nutrition counseling and care and show empathy, attentiveness, and ability to motivate communities and patients/ clients. This can be achieved notably with high-performance training programs and continuing education." ([Sayfa 5](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/9LREN8TF?page=5&annotation=YIS276FA))
