1924~ La Mar Reducing Soap was a bar of soap made with coconut oil, potassium iodide, and sassafras, marketed as a fat-reducing miracle. The product claimed to not only dissolve body fat but also tighten the skin, supposedly preventing sagging after weight loss. Each bar was priced at 50 cents, and for an extra $3, customers could buy the Slenmar Reducing Brush, a tool intended to enhance the soap’s effects. At its peak, the soap was popular, with the company selling 200 to 300 bars daily. However, in 1926, the U.S. Postmaster General labeled the product a scam and prohibited the company from advertising or selling it by mail.
