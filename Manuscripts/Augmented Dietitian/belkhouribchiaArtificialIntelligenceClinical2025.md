## 🔵 Sonuç / Conclusions (Mavi)
- "While AI offers immense promise in advancing nutritional care, its successful implementation requires clinicians to be adequately prepared to engage with these technologies. Education programs tailored to healthcare professionals, interdisciplinary collaboration between AI experts and clinicians, and robust ethical oversight are critical to ensure responsible and effective integration. By equipping clinicians with the necessary knowledge and tools, AI can serve as a powerful ally in delivering personalized and patient-centered nutritional care while maintaining human expertise at the forefront of decision-making." ([Sayfa 1](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/YCUTP8SN?page=1&annotation=TSHNMGX9))- "AI is revolutionizing clinical nutrition by introducing tools that can analyze vast datasets and generate personalized nutritional strategies. From predictive modeling in risk assessment to therapeutic decision-making support and patient monitoring, AI-based tools are set to transform how healthcare professionals diagnose, treat, and guide patients with nutritional needs. However, this transformative potential comes with significant challenges. The adoption of AI in clinical nutrition requires addressing several critical caveats, including proper education of clinicians, ensuring data quality, avoiding biases in model training, and protecting patient privacy. Human oversight remains paramount, as illustrated by the HITL principle, which emphasizes active supervision by clinicians over AI tools to ensure that their recommendations are safe, effective, and aligned with clinical judgment. For AI to be successfully integrated into clinical nutrition workflows, healthcare professionals must not only understand the capabilities of AI- based models but also recognize their limitations. This necessitates targeted educational initiatives to bridge the knowledge gap and foster a collaborative approach between clinicians and AI experts. Such cooperation is vital to unlocking AI's full potential while ensuring that patient care remains personalized, ethical, and evidence-based."
