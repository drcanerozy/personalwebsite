## 📝 4. Orijinal Özet (Abstract)
> Until two decades ago, brown adipose tissue (BAT) was studied primarily as a thermogenic organ of small rodents in the context of cold adaptation. The discovery of functional human BAT has opened new opportunities to understand its physiological role in energy balance and therapeutic applications for metabolic disorders. Signiﬁcantly, the role of BAT extends far beyond thermogenesis, including glucose and lipid homeostasis, by releasing mediators that communicate with other cells and organs. The ﬁeld has made major advances by using new model systems, ranging from subcellular studies to clinical trials, which have also led to debates. In this perspective, we identify six fundamental issues that are currently controversial and comprise dichotomous models. Each side presents supporting evidence and, critically, the necessary methods and falsiﬁable experiments that would resolve the dispute. With this collaborative approach, the ﬁeld will continue to productively advance the understanding of BAT physiology, appreciate the importance of thermogenic adipocytes as a central area of ongoing research, and realize the therapeutic potential.
