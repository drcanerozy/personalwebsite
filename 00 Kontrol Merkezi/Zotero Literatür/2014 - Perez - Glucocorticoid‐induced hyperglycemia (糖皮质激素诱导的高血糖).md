## 📝 4. Orijinal Özet (Abstract)
> Corticosteroid-induced hyperglycemia is a common medical problem that can lead to frequent emergency visits, hospital admissions and prolonged hospital stay in addition to the well known morbidity associated to hyperglycemia. The diagnosis and treatment of corticosteroid-induced hyperglycemia is, however, surprisingly undervalued by most professionals, probably due to the lack of quality studies to determine specific strategies of action.
