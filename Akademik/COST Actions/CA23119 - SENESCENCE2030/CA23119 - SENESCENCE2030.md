# ⏳ COST Action CA23119 — SENESCENCE2030
**Targeting Cell Senescence to Prevent Age-Related Diseases**

---

## 🎯 1. Çalışma Grubu Seçimi (WG2 & WG3)

* **WG2 (From Knowledge on Senescence to the Impact on Age-Related Diseases):**
  * Diyet biyoaktif bileşenleri, nutrasötikler, doğal senoterapötikler, kalori kısıtlaması, aralıklı açlık ve metabolik hastalıkların hücresel yaşlanma ekseni.
* **WG3 (The Societal Impact of Cell Senescence Research and Related Policies):**
  * Yaşlanan popülasyonda sağlıklı beslenme davranışlarının geliştirilmesi, nutrasötik odaklı klinik çalışmaların taranması ve halk sağlığı politikaları.

### 1️⃣ Scientific Background (126 Kelime — Sınır: 150)
```text
I am a faculty member and researcher in the Department of Nutrition and Dietetics. I hold a bachelor degree in nutrition and dietetics, a master degree in nutritional sciences, and a doctorate in nutrition and dietetics. My academic work focuses on clinical nutrition, personalized intermittent fasting, time restricted eating, and the Mediterranean diet. I investigate how dietary patterns and nutritional interventions influence metabolic health, obesity, and fatty liver disease. In addition, I have research experience with laboratory animal models and human dietary studies. My work explores how meal timing, caloric restriction, and dietary bioactive components affect metabolic adaptation and inflammatory processes. Through my clinical and academic background, I aim to understand practical dietary strategies that support healthy aging and improve cardiometabolic health outcomes in diverse populations.
```

### 2️⃣ Motivation (125 Kelime — Sınır: 150)
```text
My motivation for joining the SENESCENCE2030 action is to connect cellular aging research with evidence based nutrition and dietetic practice. Cellular senescence plays a critical role in age related chronic diseases, and dietary modifications represent safe and accessible interventions to support healthy aging. Participating in this COST Action will allow me to collaborate with international researchers and contribute my expertise in dietetics, fasting regimens, and metabolic health. As an academic from Turkey, I look forward to participating in working group discussions, networking events, and collaborative publications. I also wish to benefit from knowledge exchange opportunities such as short term scientific missions. Joining this network will strengthen my ongoing research on nutrition and aging while helping translate geroscience findings into realistic dietary recommendations for the public.
```

### 3️⃣ Working Group Contribution (141 Kelime — Sınır: 150)
```text
I plan to contribute actively to Working Group 2 and Working Group 3. In Working Group 2, I can contribute directly to tasks exploring nutritional and lifestyle approaches against senescence. Specifically, I can provide expertise on dietary bioactive compounds, nutraceuticals, and natural senotherapeutic agents, as well as caloric restriction and intermittent fasting regimens. I can help investigate how these dietary components influence metabolic health, chronic inflammation, and cellular aging. Additionally, I can assist in drafting collaborative literature reviews and evidence based recommendations for age related disease prevention. In Working Group 3, I can support initiatives that promote healthy nutrition behaviors and public health policies for aging populations. Furthermore, I can help review ongoing clinical trials and nutritional interventions focused on nutraceuticals and healthy longevity. I am committed to attending meetings regularly, contributing to working group deliverables, and supporting joint research activities.
```
