## 📝 4. Orijinal Özet (Abstract)
> Aging is underpinned by pronounced metabolic decline; however, the drivers remain obscure. Here, we report that IgG accumulates during aging, particularly in white adipose tissue (WAT), to impair adipose tissue function and metabolic health. Caloric restriction (CR) decreases IgG accumulation in WAT, whereas replenishing IgG counteracts CR’s metabolic beneﬁts. IgG activates macrophages via Ras signaling and consequently induces ﬁbrosis in WAT through the TGF-b/SMAD pathway. Consistently, B cell null mice are protected from aging-associated WAT ﬁbrosis, inﬂammation, and insulin resistance, unless exposed to IgG. Conditional ablation of the IgG recycling receptor, neonatal Fc receptor (FcRn), in macrophages prevents IgG accumulation in aging, resulting in prolonged healthspan and lifespan. Further, targeting FcRn by antisense oligonucleotide restores WAT integrity and metabolic health in aged mice. These ﬁndings pinpoint IgG as a hidden culprit in aging and enlighten a novel strategy to rejuvenate metabolic health.
