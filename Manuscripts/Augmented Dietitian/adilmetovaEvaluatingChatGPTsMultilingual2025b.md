Methods: "To evaluate and compare the effectiveness of the ChatGPT-4 system in providing personalized, evidence-based nutritional recommendations in English, Kazakh, and Russian in Central Asia.On the basis of 50 mock patient profiles, ChatGPT-4 generated dietary advice, and responses were evaluated for personalization, consistency, and practicality using a 5-point Likert scale. To identify significant differences between the 3 languages, the Kruskal–Wallis test was conducted."
Findings: ". The Kruskal–Wallis test indicated statistically significant differences in ChatGPT-4's performance across the 3 languages (P < 0.001). Subsequent post hoc analysis using Dunn’s test showed that the performance in both English and Russian was significantly different from that in Kazakh."
Conclusions: "Our findings show that, despite using identical prompts across 3 distinct languages, the ChatGPT-4's capability to produce sensible outputs is limited by the lack of training data in non-English languages. Thus, a customized large language model should be developed to perform better in underrepresented languages and to take into account specific local diets and practices."
Limitations: "As mentioned earlier in this manuscript, one of the limitations is the issue of “hallucination,” as we observed that ChatGPT produced responses that did not make sense or contained nonexistent words, particularly in cases involving the Kazakh language. - "Our findings show that, despite using identical prompts across 3 distinct languages, the ChatGPT-4's capability to produce sensible outputs is limited by the lack of training data in non-English languages. Thus, a customized large language model should be developed to perform better in underrepresented languages and to take into account specific local diets and practices." ## 🟠 Limitasyonlar (Turuncu)
- "As mentioned earlier in this manuscript, one of the limitations is the issue of “hallucination,” as we observed that ChatGPT produced responses that did not make sense or contained nonexistent words, particularly in cases involving the Kazakh language. This issue was mitigated by incorporating machine translation steps into the output generation process for the Kazakh language, which could result in a significant improvement in the quality of the recommendations."
