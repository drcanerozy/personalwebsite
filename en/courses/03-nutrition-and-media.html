BES 131 • Undergraduate Module (Fall)

Nutrition, Media & Science Communication

Akdeniz University Faculty of Health Sciences • Department of Nutrition and Dietetics

Course Purpose & Vision

Critical evaluation of nutrition claims in media, identifying misinformation, and translating evidence-based scientific research into accessible public communication.

🎯 Core Topics Covered: Media literacy, social media algorithms, influencer marketing, diet myths, food advertising tactics, fake news and misinformation analysis.

🎯 Course Objectives & Vision

Fosters media literacy and trains students to become effective science communicators who can counter misinformation and translate complex evidence into clear public guidance.
