Academic Profile & Research Vision

Metabolic Flexibility, Adipose Dysfunction, and Biostatistical Modeling in Cardiometabolic Disorders

I completed my Ph.D. in Nutrition and Dietetics in November 2024 at Ankara University Graduate School of Health Sciences with my doctoral dissertation titled "Evaluation of Diet-Related Risk Factors According to the Severity of Non-Alcoholic Fatty Liver Disease (NAFLD)".

My research focuses on lifespan cardiometabolic disorders, pediatric and adult obesity diagnostic metrics (Triponderal Mass Index, BMI z-scores), intermittent fasting (IF), time-restricted eating (TRE), ultra-processed foods (UPF), and systemic biomarkers (zonulin, total antioxidant capacity, oxidative stress). I employ R programming for multivariable biostatistical modeling, integrate Artificial Intelligence (AI) & LLM frameworks into biomedical data analytics, hold the Laboratory Animal Care & Use Accreditation for translational research, and actively contribute to COST Action CA23110 (INFLAMomx) focusing on multi-omics data integration in metabolic inflammation.

Pedagogy & Teaching

Undergraduate Courses

Courses taught at Akdeniz University Faculty of Health Sciences, Department of Nutrition and Dietetics:

Undergraduate

Computer and AI Applications in Nutrition & Dietetics

Biostatistical data analysis with R programming, ggplot2 visualization, AI, and computational nutrition methodology.

🎯 Core Topics: R/RStudio environment, tidyverse data wrangling, scientific publication figures with ggplot2.

💻 AI & Applications: Dietary records analysis, multivariable models, Large Language Models (LLM) for literature synthesis.

Undergraduate

Immune System and Immunonutrition

Immunological pathways, nutrient-immune interactions, microbiota modulation, and therapeutic immunonutrition protocols.

🎯 Core Topics: Innate/adaptive immunity, cytokine responses, gut barrier integrity, and mucosal immunology.

💊 Components: Glutamine, arginine, omega-3 fatty acids, nucleotides, zinc, vitamin D, and probiotics.

Undergraduate

Medical Nutrition Therapy in Adult Diseases

Pathophysiology and evidence-based clinical diet therapy in diabetes, obesity, CVD, and liver diseases.

🎯 Core Topics: Type 2 Diabetes, carbohydrate counting, dyslipidemia, NAFLD/MASLD, and renal nutrition therapy.

Undergraduate

Principles of Nutrition and Popular Diets

Energy expenditure calculations, ketogenic diets, Intermittent Fasting (IF/TRE), and popular dietary regimens.

🎯 Core Topics: Intermittent fasting protocols, ketogenic diet models, Mediterranean diet, and RCT meta-analysis.

Undergraduate

Psychosocial Aspects of Nutrition

Eating attitudes, orthorexia nervosa, emotional eating, body image, and behavioral nutrition.

🎯 Core Topics: EAT-26, ORTO-15 inventories, DEBQ emotional eating scales, and psychometric screening.

Undergraduate

Nutrition, Media & Counseling

Media literacy, nutrition communication, translating evidence to public, and clinical supervision.

🎯 Core Topics: Combating nutritional disinformation, scientific podcast design, and evidence-based blogging.
