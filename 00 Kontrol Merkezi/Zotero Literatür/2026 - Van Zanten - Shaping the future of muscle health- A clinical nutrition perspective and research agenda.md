## 📝 4. Orijinal Özet (Abstract)
> Muscle health, encompassing muscle mass, composition, strength, physical performance and patientreported outcomes, is a key determinant of clinical outcomes across the life course and a wide range of disease states. Despite growing recognition of its importance, muscle health remains insufficiently integrated into routine clinical nutrition practice, and nutritional recommendations often rely on population averages rather than individual muscle status, disease phase or metabolic context.
