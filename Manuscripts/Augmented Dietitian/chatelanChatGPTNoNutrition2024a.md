## 🔵 Sonuç / Conclusions (Mavi)
- "In a nutshell, ChatGPT a) generates texts by predicting the next word using how often the words appeared in the training data, without clinical reasoning, b) is a probabilistic model trained on texts from the Internet, where evidence-based information is marginal, and c) is not designed nor validated for delivering health information and advice. Therefore, ChatGPT has no possibility to distinguish what is supported by science or not and may convey myths, misinformation, and biases about nutrition and dietetics to users" ([Sayfa 1](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/8LZR3QBT?page=1&annotation=2R4DUURP))
