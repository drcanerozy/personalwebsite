## 📝 4. Orijinal Özet (Abstract)
> Background & aims: Impaired postprandial glucose is associated with increased risk of type 2 diabetes and all-cause mortality. Although dietary intake influences glucose response, meal-specific postprandial glucose responses under free-living conditions remain poorly characterized in adults with obesity. This cross-sectional study examined whether dietary intake at breakfast and lunch relates to subsequent postprandial glucose, and whether dinner intake relates to both postprandial and nocturnal glucose.
