Cell Discovery; https://doi.org/10.1038/s41421-025-00787-z

The concept of“pan-lipotoxicity” broadens the scientific connotation of traditional lipotoxicity. It not only focuses on the toxic effects of free fatty acids and triglycerides, but also includes the impacts of other lipid species including phospholipids, sphingolipids, and cholesterol esters, imbalances in their subcomponent proportions, lipoprotein metabolism disorders, and abnormal fat distribution and function on overall metabolic health, rather than just a single organ.
