## 📝 4. Orijinal Özet (Abstract)
> Adipocyte plasticity is fundamental to tissue remodeling and metabolic adaptability. Here we elucidate the trans-differentiation dynamics of mature lipid-laden adipocytes into dedifferentiated preadipocyte and fibroblast-like phenotypes. We demonstrate that adipocyte-derived extracellular vesicles (EVs) deliver specific miRNA cargo that modulates epithelial stem cell fate and local extracellular matrix (ECM) composition.
