Çalışma Bulguları:
The data shows a significant improvement in the three tests in the two phases. The F-value for the test phase impact was 2259.52, accompanied by a p-value significantly below 0.001, demonstrating that the AI-assisted learning approach associated with traditional note-taking skills had a robust and statistically significant influence on students results.

This shows an even greater improvement in scores from mid-test to final-test, indicating that AI-assisted note-taking strongly enhanced the students’ performance.

78% of students claimed the AI application helped them take notes.

Conclusions:
However, it should be highlighted that using AI tools only facilitates students’ learning at the initial stage and cannot replace the traditional method in the end because students should build up their solid listening comprehension skills.

Furthermore, the potential tailored learning approach should be highlighted in further research.
