## 🔴 Makale Bulguları (Kırmızı)
- "our findings provide preliminary evidence that students viewed NotebookLM as satisfactory and, in several respects, a preferable instructional resource compared to traditional textbooks. Students in our quasi-experimental study rated NotebookLM significantly more satisfactory than traditional textbooks on dimensions such as content depth, currency, affordability, and the perceived quality of supplemental materials. Likewise, they reported improved comprehension and greater engagement because of the unique functionalities provided by NotebookLM." ## 🟠 Limitasyonlar (Turuncu)
- "These limitations of NotebookLM make salient the fact that while NotebookLM offers numerous pedagogical advantages, its efficacy still depends heavily on thoughtful implementation, clear instructional guidance, and the selection of high-quality source materials. As such, educators adopting NotebookLM should frame it as a complement to educator expertise rather than a full instructional replacement."
