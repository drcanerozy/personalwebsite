## 📝 4. Orijinal Özet (Abstract)
> Every society has some institutions and establishments that perform important tasks in economical, social and cultural developments. Ranked among these important institutions, Universities have crucial duties with different dimensions in the development of societies. Universities have an important function in meeting the need for the educated experts. Reforms were made in every field as well as in universities in newly established Turkish Republic.
