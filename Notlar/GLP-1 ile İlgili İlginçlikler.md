> **Şekil Açıklaması:** GLP-1 reseptör agonizminin sağladığı santral iştah baskılanması ve gecikmiş gastrik boşalma hızlı kilo kaybı yaratır. Bu süreçte yetersiz protein alımı ve direnç egzersizi yokluğu miyosteatoz ve iskelet kası kaybına (sarkopenik obezite) yol açarken; hedefe yönelik yüksek protein ve direnç antrenmanı yağsız dokuyu korur.

The Kardashians are a family of American media personalities and entrepreneurs who rose to global prominence through reality television and have since become influential figures in popular culture. The Kardashians’ brand, called Lemme, released daily capsules called Lemme GLP-1 Daily that are proposed to contain natural supplements that result in weight loss. Three supplements are included in these capsules. They claim that the Eriomin lemon fruit extract increases GLP-1 levels and regulates blood sugar levels, the Supresa saffron extract decreases appetite and sugar cravings, and the Morosil red-orange fruit extract improves body composition via fat reduction. 7 They are marketed as the new Ozempic in pill form, 8 and are currently sold for around $70–90 per month, which is a lot less than paying for Ozempic injections out of pocket, which are around $1000 per month.
 
  Although the new Lemme pills are marketed as a magical and affordable solution for weight management, the evidence behind them is scarce. The supplements used in these pills have not been well-researched. The Eriomin lemon fruit extract has only been studied in one double-blind randomized placebo-controlled crossover trial published in 2022 with a small number of patients, 15 for each group, which showed an increase in GLP-1 levels in the blood by 17% after taking the extract for 12 weeks. 10 This is not a clinically useful endpoint, as an increase in GLP-1 levels does not necessarily mean weight reduction. The Supresa saffron extract has also only been studied in one double-blind randomized placebo-controlled study published in 2010, with 30 patients in each group, with a reduction in weight in the patient group taking the extract by a little less than 1 kg at the end of the 8 weeks. 11 Similarly, a clinical study evaluating the Morosil red-orange fruit extract in 30 patients was found to have a reduction in BMI by 1.11 ± 0.09 kg/m 2 and hip circumference by 5.96 ± 0.97 cm over 4 weeks as compared to the placebo. 12 Although these may seem like promising results, these individual studies with small numbers of patients are not generalizable. It is unclear whether any of the extracts result in significant outcomes for weight management. Since they are not well-studied or FDA-approved, they could have significant adverse effects that we are not aware of. These studies also do not define a target patient population, and outcomes regarding weight management can heavily vary depending on the background of the patients, including medical history, type of diet, and amount of exercise.
