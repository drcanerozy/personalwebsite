BES 339 • Undergraduate Module (Fall)

Principles of Nutrition and Popular Diets

Akdeniz University Faculty of Health Sciences • Department of Nutrition and Dietetics

Course Purpose & Vision

Physiological and metabolic foundations of diet planning combined with evidence-based critical analysis of current popular dietary trends.

🎯 Core Topics Covered: Evolution of human nutrition, energy expenditure models, diet culture criticism, Ketogenic diet protocols, Intermittent Fasting (IF/TRE), longevity nutrition, and dietary myths.
