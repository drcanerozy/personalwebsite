Methods: "This study aimed to assess the reliability and appropriateness of ChatGPT in creating diet plans for individuals with obesity and diet‐related non‐communicable chronic diseases. Dietary plans were generated by both a registered dietitian and ChatGPT for hypothetical individuals with identical clinical profiles and conditions. These plans were then evaluated comparatively in terms of nutritional adequacy, safety, individualization and practicality."
Findings: "ChatGPT‐generated diets demonstrated notable deficiencies in meeting specific energy and nutrient requirements and lacked personalization based on individual health needs.
