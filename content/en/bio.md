# Academic Journey & Story

## A Scientific Journey on Early Diagnosis and Biostatistical Modeling in Neurodegenerative Processes

Throughout my career, I have focused on bridging clinical medicine with data science. After completing my PhD at Hacettepe University, I worked as a postdoctoral researcher at Mayo Clinic Department of Neurologic Sciences focusing on biomarker analytics.

Currently in my laboratory, I lead international consortium projects aimed at detecting preclinical stages of Alzheimer's and Parkinson's diseases using AI-assisted imaging algorithms. I also teach graduate-level courses in *"Clinical Research Methodology"* and *"Biostatistics"*.
