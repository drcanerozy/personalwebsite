BES 317 • Undergraduate Module (Fall)

Medical Nutrition Therapy Practice I

Akdeniz University Faculty of Health Sciences • Department of Nutrition and Dietetics

Course Overview

Evidence-based clinical diet therapy and case management in cardiometabolic and gastrointestinal diseases using the Nutrition Care Process (NCP).

Core Topics

Antropometric and clinical biochemistry interpretation, obesity management, glycemic control and carbohydrate counting in Type 2 Diabetes, CVD, hypertension, Mediterranean and DASH diets, GI disorders (celiac, IBD, GERD).

Classroom Practices & AI Simulations

Comprehensive Mediterranean and DASH Diet Workshop: Cross-national portion dynamics, local biodiversity, and analytical exchange list modeling beyond simple caloric restriction (Fall 2025-2026).

Learning Outcomes

Prescribe patient-tailored medical nutrition therapies in complex metabolic conditions and monitor clinical outcomes with precision.

🎯 Course Objectives & Vision

Translates theoretical clinical pathophysiology into actionable patient management and diagnostic reasoning in adult medical nutrition therapy.
