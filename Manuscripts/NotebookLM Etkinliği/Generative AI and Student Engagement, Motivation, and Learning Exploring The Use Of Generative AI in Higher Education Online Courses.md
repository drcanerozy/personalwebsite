## 🟢 Atıf Yapılabilir (Yeşil)
- "Several studies have found that, when carefully used to provide personalized feedback or create innovative learning experiences, ChatGPT use can result in increased critical thinking (Hapsari & Wu, 2023; Guo & Lee, 2023). Significantly, however, several studies found that over-reliance on ChatGPT can hamper the development of essential skills like critical thinking, creativity, and problem-solving, unless deliberately countered (Chukwuere, 2024; Gupta, 2023; Kasneci et al., 2023). Two of the most recent studies have reached similar conclusions: Overreliance on Generative AI negatively impacts critical thinking skills and cognitive processes (Gerlich, 2025)." ## 🔴 Makale Bulguları (Kırmızı)
- "Results indicated high overall levels of student engagement (M=4.31) and perceived learning (M=4.28). However, motivation varied significantly depending on the AI tool's pedagogical positioning. Courses that used NotebookLM as a "study companion" yielded significantly higher intrinsic motivation than courses that used ChatGPT as a transactional "productivity tool." Qualitative findings revealed that AI-generated audio features (podcasts) effectively managed cognitive load, supporting Mayer’s Cognitive Theory of Multimedia Learning. Furthermore, comparisons between faculty predictions and student outcomes highlighted an "Expertise Reversal Effect," in which instructional scaffolds intended for novices were occasionally perceived as unnecessary by proficient students" (Sayfa 5) [PDF'de Gör](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/383567L4?page=5)- "The findings suggest that GenAI serves as an effective cognitive scaffold, enabling students to prevent cognitive overload while maximizing germane load processing and" (Sayfa 5) [PDF'de Gör](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/383567L4?page=5)- "functioning as the 'more capable peer' within students' Zone of Proximal Development."
