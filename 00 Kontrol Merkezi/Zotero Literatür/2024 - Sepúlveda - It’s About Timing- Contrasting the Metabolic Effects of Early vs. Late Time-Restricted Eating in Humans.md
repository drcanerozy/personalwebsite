## 📝 4. Orijinal Özet (Abstract)
> Purpose of Review Time-restricted eating (TRE), a form of intermittent fasting, restricts feeding time across the day, imposing a daily ‘eating window’. The time of day when the eating window occurs could result in differential metabolic effects. Here, we describe recent intervention studies in humans assessing the metabolic consequences of an early- (i.e., eating window starting in the early morning) vs. late (i.e., eating window starting after midday)-TRE protocol.
