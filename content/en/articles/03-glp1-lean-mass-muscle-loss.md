Rapid weight loss achieved with GLP-1 receptor agonists and dual incretins has sparked intense debate regarding muscle wasting. Clinical trials indicate that 25-40% of weight lost may come from lean mass.

This article explores the physiological nuance behind fat-free mass changes, DXA measurement limitations, mechanical unload adaptations, and actionable nutritional blueprints to protect functional muscle strength.
