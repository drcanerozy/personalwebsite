## 📝 4. Orijinal Özet (Abstract)
> Objective: Formalizing a psychological theory of attrition from very-lowcalorie diets (VLCD) and performing a first evaluation of the proposed model.
Methods: Unlike moderate hypocaloric diets, VLCD can induce mild euphoria that has inspired a psychological theory of attrition: attrition would be uniquely determined by the diet when this state is temporary during the intervention. When permanent during the diet, mild euphoria might also explain VLCD retention. Phenomena of attrition under study unfold over a dozen-week intervention and are specific to a timescale in weeks: phenomena of attrition unfolding at shorter timescales during the first week are treated as phenomena of no-start. The theory can be represented by a model made up of constructs and stimulations and inhibitions. Following the Theory Construction Methodology, the ability of the proposed model to explain single-timescale phenomena of attrition, retention, and no-start is tested.
Results: The theory-based model can be further formalized using a thermodynamic model of energy balance. A first evaluation against these phenomena is successful with causality weight values identical across individuals and shows that the proposed model could be generalized over all patients receiving the diet.
Conclusions: A theory-based model of attrition from VLCD is proposed that is based on thermodynamics.
