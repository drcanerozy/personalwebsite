## 📝 4. Orijinal Özet (Abstract)
> Conclusion There are many types of quantitative clinical study designs, and all have an important role in providing evidence for optimal clinical care. Each design has advantages and disadvantages, and although it is easy to be critical of observational studies, they are the usual ﬁrst steps on the path towards more deﬁnitive RCTs. Moreover, in many situations, RCTs are either impossible (e.g. ethically) or impractical (e.g. rare diseases).
