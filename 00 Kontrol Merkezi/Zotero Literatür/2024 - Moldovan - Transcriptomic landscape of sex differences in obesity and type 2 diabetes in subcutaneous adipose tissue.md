## 📝 4. Orijinal Özet (Abstract)
> Obesity represents a significant risk factor in the development of type 2 diabetes (T2D), a chronic metabolic disorder characterized by elevated blood glucose levels. Significant sex differences have been identified in the prevalence, development, and pathophysiology of obesity and T2D; however, the underlying molecular mechanisms remain unclear. This study aims to identify sex-specific biomarkers in obesity and T2D and enhance our understanding of the underlying mechanisms associated with sex differences by integrating expression data.
