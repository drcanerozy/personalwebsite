Another year. It passed extremely fast and yet, painfully slow. Despite developing that some think might take over our day-to-day work, data things this year. These are my favorites.  

## Inside the Confusing World of Women’s Clothing Sizes

![](https://flowingdata.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/women-clothes-size-750x579.png)

They approached the topic from several angles with 3-D models, data collection, and sizing charts. Adding to the visualization genre of variable clothes sizes, this piece helped me appreciate the process that is women’s shopping. \[[See the Project](https://www.straitstimes.com/multimedia/graphics/2025/09/womens-clothing-size-guide-singapore/index.html) / [On FlowingData](https://flowingdata.com/2025/09/23/inconsistent-womens-clothing-sizes-across-brands/)\]

## Your Zodiac Sign Is 2,000 Years Out of Date

![](https://flowingdata.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Zodiac-out-of-date-NYT-750x615.png)

The visualized shifts in time changed my perception of zodiac signs forever. I’m exaggerating. But this piece came at a welcome time during the year. \[[See the Project](https://www.nytimes.com/interactive/2025/upshot/zodiac-signs.html?unlocked_article_code=1.A1A.UEWd.TyIoTagdMaRd&smid=url-share) / [On FlowingData](https://flowingdata.com/2025/09/08/out-of-date-zodiac-signs-visually-explained/)\]

## How California fights fires from the skies

![](https://flowingdata.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Reuters-firefight-air-1090x1018.png)

Wildfires hit Southern California fast and hard. This piece illustrated, with maps, charts, and diagrams, how firefighters organized to fight the fires. As a Californian, I appreciated the timeliness. \[[See the Project](https://www.reuters.com/graphics/CALIFORNIA-WILDFIRES/AIRCRAFTS/egvbjgkajvq/) / [On FlowingData](https://flowingdata.com/2025/01/13/fighting-fire-from-the-sky/)\]

## 30 minutes with a stranger

![](https://flowingdata.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/talking-to-strangers-1090x675.png)

I am continually impressed by the connection between abstract data and reality. Chang understands and knows how to help you see the individual data points in the field of aggregates. \[[See the Project](https://pudding.cool/2025/06/hello-stranger/) / [On FlowingData](https://flowingdata.com/2025/06/20/feelings-when-strangers-talk-for-30-minutes/)\]

## Museum of All Things

![](https://flowingdata.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Museum-of-All-Things-1090x670.png)

We use Wikipedia as a reference to look up specific facts but there is also an exploration phase that sends us down rabbit holes. Claire turned the rabbit hole into an infinite museum. \[[See the Project](https://mayeclair.itch.io/museum-of-all-things) / [On FlowingData](https://flowingdata.com/2025/02/27/wikipedia-as-a-walkable-virtual-museum/)\]

## Eroding Protections for Public Lands

![](https://flowingdata.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/land-usage-reuters-1090x982.png)

The key to my visualization heart is through vintage feels that look like they came from a 19th century statistical atlas. \[[See the Project](https://www.reuters.com/graphics/USA-TRUMP/PUBLIC-LANDS/zdpxkxdwnvx/) / [On FlowingData](https://flowingdata.com/2025/07/29/usage-and-management-of-public-lands/)\]

## Swept Away

![](https://flowingdata.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/flooding-camp-mystic-NYT-1090x652.png)

A reconstruction of the flooding at Camp Mystic provided perspective on multiple levels. \[[See the Project](https://www.nytimes.com/interactive/2025/11/15/us/camp-mystic-flooding-deaths-video-map.html?unlocked_article_code=1.A1A.TXqk.X6NVUVojozfj&smid=url-share) / [On FlowingData](https://flowingdata.com/2025/11/21/visual-reconstruction-of-flooding-at-camp-mystic/)\]

## Size of Life

![](https://flowingdata.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/size-of-life-by-Agarwal-1090x741.png)

The illustrations. The music. The sound. The interaction. It all comes together to present a familiar topic, beautifully executed. \[[See the Project](https://neal.fun/size-of-life/) / [On FlowingData](https://flowingdata.com/2025/12/11/scale-of-living-things/)\]

## Bird migration is changing. What does this reveal about our planet? – visualised

![](https://flowingdata.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/bird-migration-flight-paths-1090x770.png)

Animated maps, illustrations, and sounds help you understand flight of the birds. \[[See the Project](https://www.theguardian.com/environment/ng-interactive/2025/oct/16/bird-migration-is-changing-what-does-this-reveal-about-our-planet-visualised-aoe) / [On FlowingData](https://flowingdata.com/2025/10/24/bird-migration-flight-paths/)\]

## Motorway Cycle XI

![](https://flowingdata.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Motorway-Cycle-XI-1090x1013.png)

Wilcock transforms everyday movements into music. It is one thing to sonify data and another to turn it into calming tunes. \[[See the Project](https://www.instagram.com/p/DOgHmxvCCfm/) / [On FlowingData](https://flowingdata.com/2025/09/29/sonification-of-the-everyday-through-music/)\]

Happy new year. Let’s make it a good one.
