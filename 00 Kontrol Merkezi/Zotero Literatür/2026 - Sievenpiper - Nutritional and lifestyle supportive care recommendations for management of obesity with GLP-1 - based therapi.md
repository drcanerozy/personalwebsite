## 📝 4. Orijinal Özet (Abstract)
> Background: Liraglutide, semaglutide and tirzepatide have transformed the management of obesity. However, dose-related gastrointestinal effects, obesity-associated nutritional insufficiencies, and poor long-term adherence may limit their long-term health benefits. Despite a recent joint advisory summarizing nutritional and lifestyle supportive care priorities with these therapies, there is still a significant lack of direct evidence to guide clinical practice, making consensus-based recommendations necessary.
